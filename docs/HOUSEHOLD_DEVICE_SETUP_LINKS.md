## 📋 Quick Setup Instructions for Kids' Devices

### On Apple iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open Safari and navigate to the child's direct HTTPS link above.
2. Tap the **Share button** (the square icon with the upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. The **Household Hub** app icon will now appear on their home screen.

### On Android Phone / Tablet (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome and navigate to the child's direct HTTPS link above.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu icon (⋮)** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Tap **"Install"** to confirm.
5. The standalone app icon is now installed on their home screen.
